Proposed ecosystem

Infrastructure for the recovery layer.

Five connected concepts for preparing access, responding to threats and planning for digital asset continuity.

DEVELOPMENT PREVIEWThe components below describe proposed features and product direction. They are not presented as operational functionality, completed implementations or live services.
01 / RECOVERY VAULT»

Recovery Vault

A proposed secure environment for establishing recovery arrangements and protecting eligible digital assets through predefined user preferences and conditions.

02 / GUARDIAN NETWORK»

Guardian Network

A trusted authorization framework that could allow designated guardians to participate in predefined recovery procedures without holding unilateral control.

03 / EMERGENCY SHIELD»

Emergency Shield

A proposed emergency protection mechanism designed to help users respond to compromised wallets, suspicious activity and time-sensitive security events.

04 / LEGACY TRANSFER»

Legacy Transfer

A digital inheritance framework for establishing secure asset transfer arrangements with clear authorization and continuity conditions.

05 / RECOVERY PROTOCOL»

Recovery Protocol

A structured recovery process using cryptographic verification, authorization requirements and predefined security conditions to make recovery deliberate and verifiable.
