About RECLAIMR

Ownership should be built to endure.

RECLAIMR is an independent project exploring infrastructure for safer access, structured recovery and more resilient digital asset ownership.

Our mission

Make digital ownership safer.

The mission

To build infrastructure that makes digital asset ownership safer by addressing the challenges of access loss, wallet security and asset recovery.

The problem we're solving

Traditional crypto wallets place a high degree of responsibility on the individual. When private keys are lost, compromised or inaccessible, users can have few meaningful options. RECLAIMR is exploring systems that add carefully designed recovery and protection layers around that reality.

The vision

A crypto ecosystem where losing access does not automatically mean losing ownership of digital assets — supported by clear authorization, security conditions and user-defined continuity planning.

How we think

Infrastructure over promises.

RECLAIMR is presented as a product vision and development preview. We distinguish planned concepts from operational functionality and avoid claiming completed integrations, audits or launches.

Cryptographic verification

Proposed recovery flows would use verification and authorization requirements to make sensitive actions deliberate and accountable.

Smart contract systems

Smart contracts are being considered as a programmable foundation for predefined conditions, permissions and recovery logic.

Security mechanisms

Protection is treated as a continuous layer: monitoring, suspicious activity response and emergency safeguards working alongside recovery planning.

The RECLAIMR direction

Build for the moment access becomes uncertain.
